# 🛒 Madhav Ecommerce Analysis Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](madhav-ecommerce-dashboard.png)

A Power BI dashboard built to track ecommerce sales, profit trends, and customer behavior across India.

---

## 1. 🎯 Project Objective

Analyze ecommerce sales data to uncover revenue trends, top-performing categories, and customer spending patterns — enabling management to make faster, smarter business decisions.

---

## 2. 📦 Dataset Overview

| Table | Description |
|---|---|
| Orders | Transaction records with amount, quantity & payment mode |
| Customers | Customer names and purchase history |
| Products | Category, sub-category, and profit data |
| Location | State-wise sales distribution across India |

---

## 3. ⚙️ Process

- Imported and cleaned raw ecommerce data in Power BI
- Created calculated columns and DAX measures for KPIs
- Built interactive visuals with quarter-based slicers
- Designed a dark-themed dashboard for clear presentation

---

## 4. ❓ Business Questions & KPIs Solved

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| What is the total revenue generated? | **₹438K** Sum of Amount |
| What is the overall profit? | **₹37K** Sum of Profit |
| How many units were sold? | **5,615** units |
| Which state drives the most sales? | **Maharashtra** leads all states |
| Which category sells the most? | **Clothing** — 63% of quantity |
| Which payment mode is most preferred? | **COD** — 44% of orders |
| Which sub-category is most profitable? | **Printers** top profit contributor |
| Which months show a loss? | **June & July** recorded negative profit |

---

## 5. 📊 Dashboard Insights

- 👗 **Clothing dominates** with 63% of total quantity sold — key category to prioritize
- 🖨️ **Printers** are the highest profit sub-category, followed by Bookcases and Sarees
- 💳 **COD (44%)** is the most used payment mode — UPI (21%) is growing fast
- 📍 **Maharashtra & Madhya Pradesh** are the top revenue-generating states
- 📉 **June–July dip** signals a seasonal low — a good window for promotions

---

## 6. 💡 Project Insights

- Nearly half of customers prefer Cash on Delivery, suggesting trust barriers with digital payment — targeted UPI incentives could shift this
- Clothing volume is high but profit per unit may be thin — Electronics and Printers likely carry better margins
- Q1 and Q4 consistently outperform mid-year quarters, indicating seasonal demand peaks

---

## 7. ✅ Final Conclusion

The Madhav Ecommerce store is revenue-strong but has profit gaps in mid-year months. Focusing on high-margin sub-categories like Printers, pushing digital payments, and running targeted campaigns in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh will directly improve profitability and growth.
